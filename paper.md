# The Date of the Book of Revelation — Draft

## Research question

Was the Book of Revelation written during the reign of Nero (60s AD) or the
reign of Domitian (90s AD)? What external (patristic) and internal evidence
bears on the question?

## Outline

1. The two traditional dating positions
2. External evidence (patristic testimony)
3. Internal evidence (imperial cult persecution, state of the churches)
4. Assessment

## Draft text

### 1. The two traditional dating positions

Scholarship on Revelation's date splits into two main camps: a Domitianic
date (mid-90s AD) and a Neronic date (mid-60s AD). See `../README.md`
Section 3 of the department manual for why every claim below is marked with
its fact-check status rather than stated as settled.

### 2. External evidence (patristic testimony)

The strongest external witness for the Domitianic date is Irenaeus of Lyons,
*Against Heresies* 5.30.3 (c. 180 AD), who dates John's vision to "the end
of Domitian's reign" [fact-check: see TODO.md]. Clement of Alexandria's
account in *Quis Dives Salvetur* 42 is sometimes read as indirectly
supporting the same dating [fact-check: see TODO.md — flagged as needing a
specialist read, not just a lookup].
