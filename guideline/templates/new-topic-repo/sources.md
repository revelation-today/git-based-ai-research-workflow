# Sources

Bibliography for this topic. Add a source only once you have actually
verified the claim(s) it is being cited for — an AI-suggested source that
turns out not to exist, or not to say what was claimed, does not belong
here (see `ai-requests/` for the record of what the AI suggested).

Suggested fields per entry: author, title, year, publisher/journal,
page(s), and a one-line note on which claim(s) in `paper.md` it supports.
